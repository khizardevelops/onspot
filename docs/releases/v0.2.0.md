# onspot 0.2.0

Groq requests that stay within their limits, clearer correction cards, a faster interface, steadier
on-device speech, a logo, and the first macOS build.

## Highlights

- **Groq within its limits.** Requests are paced to each model's per-minute limits and move to
  another free model when one is busy (Settings → LLM → When a model is busy).
- **Clearer cards.** "Did you mean to say?" can list only the fixed passages, numbered. Dropped
  words are struck through. Translations are made only when you ask and can be hidden. Word
  meanings load per section.
- **Compact feedback cards**, tinted by how serious each point is.
- **A faster interface.** Closed menus, closed Settings rows and cards out of reach are built only
  when needed, and the Feedback panel slides without reflowing the page.
- **Steadier on-device speech.** Long texts are spoken sentence by sentence, the clip you are
  listening to comes before clips prepared ahead, and the speech worker shuts down after 90 seconds
  idle. Clips are kept below full volume so they no longer distort.
- **Recording on more browsers.** Where the browser cannot record Opus directly, onspot records
  through WebCodecs instead.
- **A logo and app icon**, used in the app, the favicon and the desktop and Android icons.
- **macOS and Flatpak.** The first macOS build (Apple Silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) and a Flatpak
  for Linux. The AppImage now carries its own audio plugins and is test-started on Ubuntu 22.04 and
  Arch before release.

## Known issues

- **French only.**
- **The installers are not code-signed.** On macOS, you may need to allow the app in
  **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. On Windows, SmartScreen may ask you to confirm.
- **Recording stops at 60 seconds.**
- **No iOS build.**
- **Windows and macOS builds are produced by CI and have not had a full hands-on pass.** Please
  report anything that looks wrong.
