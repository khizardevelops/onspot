# onspot 0.2.4

A setup page for new learners, optional practice reminders, transcript export, more depth in dark
mode, a bigger app icon, and onspot as an installable web app for iPhone and iPad.

## Highlights

- **First-run setup.** A new setup page walks you through connecting your coach (the API key) and
  choosing how corrections sound. Reopen it any time from Settings.
- **Practice reminders.** Optional and off by default: a nudge about daily or every three days,
  shown in Practice and as a system notification where your device allows it.
- **Download notices.** onspot tells you when language data finishes downloading, or when the
  download fails.
- **Export a session.** Save a session's transcript and corrections as Markdown, from History or
  Practice.
- **What onspot can help with.** Settings explains what onspot can and cannot do for you.
- **Word-for-word pairs** now read left to right.
- **Depth in dark mode.** Surfaces step in brightness and catch the light instead of relying on
  shadows, and the translation note has a header. The background glare follows the mode:
  blue-green in casual, red in exam.
- **A bigger app icon.** The new mark drops the black tile, so it no longer looks small in docks,
  launchers and taskbars. Every installer, the Android icon, the Flatpak and the favicon use it.
- **iPhone and iPad (preview).** onspot installs as a web app from
  <https://onspot.khizardevelops.workers.dev>: open it in Safari, then **Share → Add to Home
  Screen**. The app shell works offline and tells you when a new version is ready.

## First run

1. The **setup page** opens on first launch: connect your coach with a
   [Groq](https://console.groq.com) key (the free tier is enough to try it), or point onspot at a
   local OpenAI-compatible server.
2. For on-device speech, go to **Settings → Language data → Download.** French needs about 363 MB,
   once.
3. Allow the **microphone**.
4. Pick a prompt and start talking.

## Known issues

- **French only.** Japanese is in development and is hidden in production builds until it passes a
  human listening test and transcript review.
- **The installers are not code-signed yet.** On macOS, you may need to allow the app in
  **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. On Windows, SmartScreen may ask you to confirm. On
  Android, you may need to allow installs from your browser or file manager.
- **Recording stops at 60 seconds.** Longer takes are on the roadmap.
- **The iPhone and iPad web app has not been tested on a real device yet.** Cloud speech (Groq) is
  the recommended setup there, and Google Drive sync is not set up yet on the new web address.
- **Windows and macOS builds are produced by CI and have not had a full hands-on pass** by the
  maintainer, who develops on Linux and Android. Please report anything that looks wrong.
