# onspot 0.2.2

Swipe gestures and a Back button on phones, and installers that carry the onspot icon and artwork.

## Highlights

- **Swipe on phones.** Swipe the Feedback sheet down to close it, past a third of the way or with a
  flick. The stats sheet swipes down and the timeline swipes right. Swipe sideways to step through
  the Feedback filters and the Settings tabs. Scrolling, text fields, sliders and long-press word
  selection still work as before.
- **A Back button** on every page except Practice. It and Android's Back button close whatever is
  open on top first, then go to the previous page.
- **Branded installers.** The Windows setup and `.msi`, the macOS disk image and the Linux packages
  now show the onspot icon and artwork instead of the installer tools' defaults.
- **Sharper icons.** Android has an adaptive icon, with a monochrome layer for themed icons. The
  Linux window and menu icons are no longer blurry.

## Known issues

- **French only.**
- **The installers are not code-signed.** On macOS, you may need to allow the app in
  **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. On Windows, SmartScreen may ask you to confirm. On
  Android, you may need to allow installs from your browser or file manager.
- **Recording stops at 60 seconds.**
- **No iOS build.**
- **Windows and macOS builds are produced by CI and have not had a full hands-on pass.** Please
  report anything that looks wrong.
